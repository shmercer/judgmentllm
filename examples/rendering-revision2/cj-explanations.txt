## Writing Task

The following writing task was provided to students. The time limit was 15 minutes.

“The internet makes traveling unnecessary.”

What do you think?

Write an argumentative essay in English, in which you discuss the statement.

## Comparison Criterion

### Overall Writing Quality
Criterion ID: `overall_writing_quality`

Compare how clearly each essay states and supports its position on the writing task.

## Essays

essay_01:
The internet can replace some journeys, but it cannot replace every experience. A video call lets relatives speak without a long flight.

However, visiting a city also means hearing its streets and meeting people outside a screen. Online information helps us choose a journey; it does not make every journey unnecessary.

essay_02:
The internet shows many places. I can see a museum online and I do not have to buy a ticket.

Travel takes time. Therefore the internet makes traveling unnecessary.

## Output Format

Return only a JSON object containing "winner_id" and "explanation".

The value of "winner_id" must be exactly "essay_01" or "essay_02", identifying the essay that better fulfills the comparison criterion.

Ties are not allowed.

Provide exactly one concise sentence explaining how specific evidence in the essays supports the judgment. Give a judgment justification rather than improvement advice.

Do not include additional fields.
{"type":"object","properties":{"winner_id":{"enum":["essay_01","essay_02"]},"explanation":{"type":"string","minLength":1}},"required":["winner_id","explanation"],"additionalProperties":false}
