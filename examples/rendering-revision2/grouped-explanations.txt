## Writing Task

The following writing task was provided to students. The time limit was 15 minutes.

“The internet makes traveling unnecessary.”

What do you think?

Write an argumentative essay in English, in which you discuss the statement.

## Rubric

Use only the stated descriptors.

### 1. Introductory Sentence
Trait ID: `introductory_sentence`

**Score 2**

There is an introductory sentence (or several), which leads to the essay’s topic.

**Score 1**

Any one of the following:

- There is an introductory sentence, but it does not align with the topic of the writing prompt.
- There is an introductory sentence, but it does not align with the rest of the essay.
- There is an introductory sentence, but it does not align with the topic or the rest of the essay.

**Score 0**

Criterion not yet fulfilled.

### 2. Supporting Evidence (synthetic illustration)
Trait ID: `synthetic_support`

Assess the support for the stated position.

**Score 2**

A supporting example is provided and connected to the stated position.

**Score 1**

A supporting example is provided.

**Score 0**

No supporting example is provided.

## Student Essay

The internet can replace some journeys, but it cannot replace every experience. A video call lets relatives speak without a long flight.

However, visiting a city also means hearing its streets and meeting people outside a screen. Online information helps us choose a journey; it does not make every journey unnecessary.
