Focus primarily on the text's deep structure: how clearly it introduces the topic and presents a position, develops and supports arguments, addresses counterarguments and rebuttals, and organizes and connects ideas across the introduction, main body, and conclusion. Ignore spelling, grammar, punctuation, and other surface features as much as possible, except when they substantially interfere with understanding.

Take a holistic approach: Which text makes a better overall impression? Use the guidance as considerations, not a checklist. Judge how effectively the elements work together rather than scoring or counting individual features.
